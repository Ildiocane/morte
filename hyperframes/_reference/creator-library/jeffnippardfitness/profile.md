# @jeffnippardfitness

**Platforms:** TikTok @jeffnippardfitness · YouTube @JeffNippard · Instagram @jeffnippard
**Niche:** Evidence-based hypertrophy and strength training: studies, experiments and tier lists, explained with real lifting footage.
**Why the user saves them:** Luca (fitness PT) wants his videos to feel like Jeff's: credible, science-backed, visually clear explainers instead of hype edits.

## Signature vibe
Calm, confident "science teacher in a premium gym." Every claim is backed up on screen: a study page with a highlighter, a chart that builds bar by bar, or a color-coded overlay on the muscle or movement. Edits are clean (mostly hard cuts, simple white captions). The production value comes from purposeful, consistent motion graphics layered on top of high-quality footage.
